<div class="ab-hello">Hi, I'm Azim 👋</div>
<div class="ab-headline">From deep multimodal representation learning to <span>building virtual cells</span>.</div>
<p>I am a Principal AI Scientist working at the intersection of artificial intelligence, machine learning, and computational biology. I build foundation models and AI systems that integrate multimodal biological data, including genomics, transcriptomics, proteomics, imaging, and scientific literature, to accelerate scientific discovery and therapeutic innovation.</p>
<p>My work spans large language models, generative biology, perturbation modeling, and agentic AI systems that reason over complex biological processes. I am especially excited about <strong>Virtual Cells</strong> and <strong>biological world models</strong> that simulate cellular behavior, predict responses to genetic and chemical perturbations, and guide experimental design at scale.</p>
<p>Over the past decade, I have led AI-driven solutions across drug discovery, translational research, and biopharmaceutical R&amp;D. My goal is to transform how science is done by combining foundation models, autonomous agents, and mechanistic biological knowledge into intelligent systems that accelerate the path from data to therapies.</p>

<div class="ab-section">👤 Short Bio</div>

<div class="ab-bio">
<p>I am currently a Principal Artificial Intelligence Scientist at <a href="https://www.jnj.com/">J&amp;J</a>, working on AI/ML for multimodal data integration (omics, images, text, etc.), generative biology, and perturbational modeling.</p>
<p>I received a master's in Bioinformatics from the <a href="https://www.mpi-inf.mpg.de/home/">Max Planck Institute for Informatics</a> and <a href="https://zbi-www.bioinf.uni-sb.de/en">Saarland University</a> in Germany, and did my PhD in Computer Science with the <a href="https://www.imprs-cs.de/">International Max Planck Research School for Computer Science</a> at the Max Planck Institute for Informatics, where I was fortunate to be advised by <a href="https://schulzlab.github.io/MS.html">Prof. Marcel Schulz</a>. I was also a visiting student in <a href="http://fraenkel.mit.edu/">Prof. Ernest Fraenkel's lab</a> at MIT.</p>
<p>From April 2019 to February 2021, I was a postdoctoral research associate at the <a href="https://www.broadinstitute.org/">Broad Institute of MIT and Harvard</a> and <a href="https://www.csail.mit.edu/">MIT CSAIL</a> under the supervision of <a href="http://compbio.mit.edu/compbio.html">Prof. Manolis Kellis</a>.</p>
<p>⚽ In my spare time, I play soccer and go hiking. My favorite soccer player is <a href="https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=OUKGsb8CpF8&amp;t=412s">Cristiano Ronaldo</a>.</p>
